Guide

1. Record Sale: Product + Amount + Customer, tap Record. Receipt shows, tap Send to WhatsApp.

2. Inventory: Add Product + Cost + Sell. Profit shows automatically. Tap Sell to sell fast.

3. Debtors: Method = Debt. Person appears in Debtors with Owing for X days. Edit message, tap Send.

4. Barcode: Tap Scanner, point camera, it beeps, shows code, tap Use This Code.

5. Shop Owner: Save Shop Name. Receipt will come from that shop name.

6. Admin: Only you see it. It shows EVERYONE who signed up.

About Luxora

Created & Hosted by Gideon Uvomata

LUXORA Sales Manager was created by Gideon Uvomata to help small business owners, shop owners, provision store owners and market traders.

Many traders lose money because they write sales on paper. LUXORA solves it.

What Luxora Does:

- Record daily sales with customer name
- Track who is owing you (Debtors auto)
- Manage Inventory - 48 items support
- Barcode Scanner with beep sound
- Records filter - Today, Yesterday, This Week, This Month, This Year, All Time
- Know your profit and debt daily

How it helps society:

- No more paper loss
- Helps traders grow business
- Works on phone, no laptop needed
- Each business has private account
